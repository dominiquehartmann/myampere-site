Senec.Home V3 and V2.1

Not official manufacturer information: compiled by the OpenAmpere contributors, can be incomplete or wrong. No affiliation with Senec or EKD; not legal advice. See the device knowledge README.

Applies to: Senec.Home V3 hybrid, V3 hybrid duo (also LFP) and V2.1 with their local web interface · Sold as: Senec storage systems (EKD sold them until about 2022) · OpenAmpere: driver senec (src/openampere/drivers/senec/), "register map" senec_lala · Support: display only · Confirmed on a real device: no (built from open projects and the simulator)

Summary

  • Not Modbus: the device has a local web interface, lala.cgi. A POST of JSON {"SECTION": {"KEY": ""}} returns the same JSON with the values filled in as typed hex strings.
  • HTTPS with a self-signed certificate since about firmware 825 (2023), plain HTTP before and on the V2.1.
  • The owner chooses "Senec" in the setup (advanced settings); detection over Modbus and the network scan do not find it.
  • No energy history: firmware 825 emptied the statistics section, so there are no lifetime counters.
  • Asking too often is said to restart the device or stop its cloud sync: OpenAmpere asks at most every 30 s.
  • Senec V4, P4 and E4 have no local web interface and are out of scope.

Decisions in OpenAmpere

DecisionWhyIssue/PR
Display only: every write method refuses, supports_control is off.Nothing confirmed on a device; the known writes have no watchdog.#521
Only when the owner chooses "Senec"; port 502 (the setup's default) stands for 443 and then 80, HTTPS before HTTP. Its error messages name the web interface, not Modbus.It does not answer on the Modbus port, so the other drivers' detection would stop there.#521
The certificate is not checked, and not pinned after the first contact either (as #521 first planned).It is self-signed with nothing to check it against. A pinned certificate would lock the owner out after every firmware update or reset that brings a new one, with no way to pair again in the app; and what an attacker in the home network could gain is only wrong values on the display: OpenAmpere only reads and sends nothing secret.#521
Asked at most every 30 s (MIN_POLL_S), also after an error (no quick retries; the reconnect waits at least as long), one request at a time, below 600 characters, the session cookie kept until a reconnect.What the open projects found the device stands.#521
Error answers of the device (403, 404) are closed at once.Unclosed, they hold a connection to the device until the error object is collected.#540
No counters: no energy history for this device.The device has none since firmware 825; integrating the power is a follow-up.#521
The diagnostics show the ENERGY values of the last read with Senec's own signs (powers rounded) and how the device answered (scheme, port), not the device id; the report sends no request of its own.So the first owner's report can confirm the signs.#521
A redirect of the web interface (301/302/303) is not followed; it counts as an error answer and is closed at once: during the detection the device answers but is no Senec, while reading it is not answering.Requests stay at the address the owner set, like every other outgoing request (outbound.py).#548
No search for a new address when the device is unreachable, and the device query (Mehr → Geräteabfrage) does not read it: it says so.Both read Modbus registers only; this device has none. A fixed IP address in the router keeps it reachable.#547

Open questions

  • Everything: no owner has tried it yet. Whether a V2.1 answers on HTTP or HTTPS, and whether newer firmware still answers lala.cgi at all.
  • The signs of GUI_BAT_DATA_POWER and GUI_GRID_POW on a real device.
  • Which polling interval is safe next to the Senec cloud sync.
  • What STAT_STATE reports in a power cut (for the outage detection).
