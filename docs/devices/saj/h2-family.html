SAJ H2, HS2, AS2 (family range of type codes)

Not official manufacturer information: compiled by the OpenAmpere contributors, can be incomplete or wrong. Changing inverter settings can affect the warranty and grid rules; no affiliation with SAJ or EKD; not legal advice. See the device knowledge README.

Applies to: SAJ hybrid inverters H2, HS2 and AS2, every type code 0x0050–0x006F in register 0x8F00 · Sold as: "Ampere.StoragePro" (H2, HS2 and the three-phase 0x005E; for the AS2 not stated in the sources) · OpenAmpere: driver saj (src/openampere/drivers/saj/), register map saj_h2 · Support: display for the whole family range; control (battery settings, export limit, grid charging) for 0x005A–0x005E since 0.29.0 · Confirmed on a real device: partly (identification, battery limits and battery temperature; in daily use on a 0x005E; the signs of battery and grid power not yet; of OpenAmpere's own writes only the on-grid depth 0x3644 on a 0x005E)

Type-specific page: 0x005E, "3P-X E2".

Summary

  • One register map for the whole family; the type code in 0x8F00 decides the name and whether control is enabled. Unknown codes in the family range are read like an H2.
  • The live and counter registers come from community projects; the control registers from SAJ's "H2-3~6K-S2 single phase communication protocol" (2022-12-02). The battery limits 0x3644/0x3645 were matched with the manufacturer's app on a real device (#334).
  • Three-phase types have summed grid counters (0x4167 ff.); some firmware lacks them, then the L1 counters are used.
  • SAJ has no remote control with a watchdog. Passive mode stays on until it is changed, so OpenAmpere charges from the grid through a short charge slot of the time-of-use mode (slot 7) that it keeps extending; if OpenAmpere stops, charging ends with the slot, at most 10 minutes later.
  • The work mode "backup" has its own reserve (0x3651); the reserve of normal operation (0x3644) does not apply there.
  • Still open: the signs of battery and grid power, and whether the PV counter covers all MPPTs.

Registers

All reads use FC03, all writes FC06 (one register at a time). Device address (unit id) 1 or 2, port 502. "Community" means the projects listed under Sources.

The limit is written before the mode; if 0x365C is not 1 or 3 it is set to 1. Grid charging through a short charge slot of the time-of-use mode, not passive mode. The issue planned slot 1; the implementation uses slot 7 (0x3618–0x361A, bit 6 of 0x3604).Passive mode would keep charging after OpenAmpere stops; a slot ends by itself. Slot 7 leaves the owner's slots (usually the first) alone. This is the documented exception to "continuous control only via a remote control with a watchdog" (AGENTS.md).#377, #378
The slot covers today from now for 10 minutes (never past 23:59; the next day gets a new slot) and is extended when fewer than 5 minutes are left; only the end moves, so a stored register is written about every 5 minutes. Power = rated power × % rounded up, 1–100 %.If OpenAmpere stops, charging ends at most 10 minutes later; few writes to stored registers.#377, #378
Start order: slot registers, 0x365F = 1, bit 6 of 0x3604, AppMode 1 last. With every command, 0x365F, the bit and AppMode are set again where they differ, and a slot changed while switched off is written again.The slot holds OpenAmpere's values before it is switched on; like the FoxESS remote control, OpenAmpere takes over again after another device changed something.#378, #382, #383
The owner's AppMode, 0x365F and slot-7 words are kept in the remote_command meta from the start of a session (also when someone changes them in between) and put back when charging ends, also after a restart. A work mode the owner chooses while OpenAmpere charges is not written but kept for the end of charging, and stored in the meta right away (#493). After a restart, before the first charging command, the kept values are taken over from the meta while slot 7 still holds OpenAmpere's words, is on and AppMode is 1; otherwise the new mode is written right away (#497). The takeover also happens when the app reads the settings, so it shows the owner's mode instead of none (#500).Before #383 a slot switched off by another device made the next command look like a new start, AppMode 1 was stored as the owner's mode and the inverter stayed in time-of-use after charging.#378, #382, #383
Slot 7 is never taken over while it is switched on with other values (the app says to free it in the SAJ app). Release only while slot 7 still holds OpenAmpere's values: clear bit 6, put back AppMode (if 1), 0x365F and the owner's slot words, then check that bit 6 is off.Release only what OpenAmpere started.#378, #383
OpenAmpere's new slot words are kept before they are written. While a slot write may have been cut off (written, but the answer timed out), each slot word may be the one before or OpenAmpere's new one and the slot still counts as OpenAmpere's; the remote_command meta keeps this also after a failed command, so a release after a restart puts the owner's slot back as well.A write that reached the inverter without an answer would otherwise leave a weekly grid charge switched on.#491
The inverter clock (0x4000) is read before each charging command; an implausible clock (no valid date) stops the command. The issue planned to refuse a clock that is far off; the code does not compare it with real time.The slot follows the inverter's clock.#377, #378
AppMode 3 (passive) is reported as "another device controls the inverter".Only external controllers use passive mode.#378
The charging page says that charging can go on up to 10 minutes after OpenAmpere stops, and that the slot repeats weekly until OpenAmpere runs again or it is deleted in the manufacturer's app.The owner must know what stays on the inverter.#378
0x8300–0x8306 is not used.Undocumented and untested.#377
Diagnostics check "Steuerung (SAJ)": slot 7 on/off, its length and power (no times or days), 0x365F, 0x365A/0x365C and whether the clock is readable (not the time).Times and days would tell when the owner charges.#378

Open questions

  • Sign of the battery power 0x40A6 (+ = discharging per evcc) and of the grid power 0x40AD (+ = import per evcc) in raw register values of a real device; so far only seen in the app (#576).
  • Why OpenAmpere could read a 0x005E next to the IQ box on one installation (#576) but not on another (#469): the number of Modbus TCP connections the inverter accepts (an owner in #419 reported two), other clients using them, or the box's own connection habits.
  • What 0x40A0 computes, and why it is off on some devices while the battery runs (#576).
  • Whether the PV energy counter 0x40BF/0x40C5 covers all MPPTs (registers.md).
  • Whether 0x8F01 is the rated power in W on every type: diagnostics reports from devices of different power classes would settle it (#492).
  • Whether 0x365A works as an export limit at all on a real device: two unverified comments in the Home Assistant community thread on the SAJ H2 integration say it seemed not to limit, or had an odd scaling (#492).
  • Size of the export limit step of 0x365A on a real device (per mille of 0x8F01, derived), and which value 0x365C has on EKD units (#377).
  • Whether extending a running charge slot keeps charging without a gap (#377, #378).
  • Behaviour of 0x3651 in backup mode: does the battery really supply the house down to 0x3651 and ignore 0x3644 (#380, #381)?
  • Meaning of 0x402C: a diagnostics report in which the off-grid depth, 0x3651 and 0x3644 all differ would settle it; meaning of 0x4029 and 0x402B (#334).
  • OpenAmpere's writes to 0x3645–0x3647, 0x3651, 0x365A/0x365C and the charge slot are not yet confirmed on a real device (#378); writing 0x3644 was confirmed on a 0x005E, whether the battery keeps to it is open (#601).
  • Which lowest off-grid limit an SAJ accepts; if a device rejects 0 %, the limit must come from the driver (#69).
  • Settled: the old registers.md said 0x3644/0x3645 are not in a public SAJ protocol and that there is none for the H2/HS2; both were written before the H2 protocol was found during #377. The protocol lists 0x3644 (on-grid discharge depth), 0x3645 (off-grid discharge depth), 0x3646 (charge limit) and 0x3647 (AppMode). The measurement registers have not been compared with it one by one.
  • The clock check refuses only an invalid date; a clock that is far off from real time is not detected, although #377 planned it.
  • Whether the AS2 (0x0055, 0x0056) answers the same map, and whether it could get control.
  • Whether 0x8300–0x8306 is a remote control that ends by itself (untested, stanus74/home-assistant-saj-h2-modbus#18).
