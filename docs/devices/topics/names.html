Sales names and manufacturer models

Not official manufacturer information. Compiled by the OpenAmpere contributors; can be incomplete or wrong. No affiliation with the manufacturers named here or with EKD, not legal advice. See Device knowledge.

Applies to: the names under which EKD sold storage systems, the manufacturer models behind them and the OpenAmpere driver for each, and the hardware EKD sold since 2018 · Sold as: see the tables · OpenAmpere: drivers foxess (register maps foxess_h3_new, foxess_h3_legacy) and saj (register map saj_h2) · Support: display and control, see the device pages · Confirmed on a real device: partly

All product and company names are trademarks of their owners and are used here only to describe which devices OpenAmpere works with.

Summary

  • The storage systems EKD sold under its own "Ampere" name since 2023 are standard hybrid inverters of FoxESS and SAJ; OpenAmpere talks to them directly with the same drivers as to devices from other sources.
  • Before that, EKD sold storage systems of other makers (Senec, then Solarmax as "AMPERE.Storage", M-TEC, Enphase). OpenAmpere supports none of them yet; see Hardware sold by EKD over time.
  • "Ampere.StoragePro E3" is a FoxESS H3; which of its two register maps the E3 uses is still open.
  • The older "Ampere.StoragePro", including the "E2", is an SAJ of the H2/HS2 family; the E2 reports type code 0x005E.
  • "Ampere.IQ" is EKD's app, which works only through EKD's servers; the "IQ box" or "smartbox" is the energy manager installed with the systems (iq-box.md).
  • On the "Ampere.StoragePro" systems sold by EKD since 2023 (SAJ and FoxESS), Modbus TCP is already turned on; on the older systems of other makers it depends on the device (a Senec has no Modbus TCP at all).

Behaviour

FactStatusSource
On the "Ampere.StoragePro" systems sold by EKD since 2023 (SAJ and FoxESS), Modbus TCP is already turned on; owners do not need to switch it on. This does not hold for the older systems of other makers.derived#84, #85; README
EKD stands for Energiekonzepte Deutschland GmbH. It and other companies of its group filed for insolvency in early October 2026; the proceedings of seven companies run at the local court of Leipzig.documented (public insolvency announcements)#85, #91, #92; README
EKD was founded in 2018 by the founder of Senec and sold Senec storage systems at first; until 2022 its website called it a Senec partner.documentedWikipedia, "Energiekonzepte Deutschland"; pv magazine, 2022-06-16
EKD became majority owner of Solarmax in 2022; Solarmax filed for insolvency in late 2024, before the EKD group.documentedpv magazine, 2022-06-16; pv magazine, 2024-12-06
EKD switched off the storage function of "AMPERE.Storage" remotely in September 2025 and released it again after a fuse was fitted on site.confirmed (owner reports)iq-box.md; Photovoltaikforum thread on the shutdown
Senec stopped taking new customers in July 2026; app, service and the exchange of battery modules go on for existing systems.documented (trade press)ess-news, 2026-07-14
Energieversum, which also sold Senec storage, is a separate installer, not an EKD brand.derivedcompany websites
"Ampere" is a sales name; the devices come from the makers in the tables above. The datasheets of the "Ampere.StoragePro E3" (2026-05) name AMPERE German Electric Innovation GmbH as maker ("Hersteller") although the device is a FoxESS; the 2023 datasheets of the SAJ-based "AMPERE.StoragePro" carry EKD's copyright instead.documentedamperesolar.de datasheet "Ampere.StoragePro E3 12 kW", 2026-05; EKD datasheets "AMPERE.Storage.Pro", 2023-04, and "AMPERE.StoragePro", 2023-07

Decisions in OpenAmpere

DecisionWhyIssue/PR
The model string is only a hint; the driver checks which register map really answers and prefers the newer one. Owners can choose the map by hand.Sales names and model strings do not tell the firmware's register map.docs/devices.md
Detection does not depend on a sales name: FoxESS at unit 247 first, then SAJ at 1 and 2, then Solarmax at 1, then M-TEC at 252, 255 and 247 (modbus.md).The same devices are sold under several names.docs/devices.md
App texts call the box the "former smartbox", without a manufacturer name; the README names companies only in the background and no-affiliation notes.Names only descriptively.#53
A driver for a storage system EKD sold must never switch on a storage function that EKD switched off remotely (as with "AMPERE.Storage" in 2025); at most it reads.The lock was announced as a safety retrofit; switching it off is the safe direction.#514, #515

Open questions

  • Which register map (newer or older firmware) the "Ampere.StoragePro E3" uses, and which H3 variant it is. The E3 is a FoxESS H3 (#311), but its map is still open in registers.md (#185).
  • Whether every "Ampere.StoragePro" system has Modbus TCP turned on is known only from owners' reports, not from a document.
  • Whether EKD sold Senec.Home V2.1 in 2018–2019; the web archive has no copy of EKD's website before December 2021.
  • Which PV inverters EKD installed next to a Senec, and whether the Senec reads a Fronius over the Datamanager's Modbus RTU or over Solar Net (only with Solar Net would Modbus TCP stay free).
  • Whether "AMPERE.Storage" ships with Modbus TCP on, or only after "Connectivity+"; what the fuse fitted in 2025 protects against, and whether systems without it are still locked.
  • Which Enphase models EKD installed, and when EKD stopped selling M-TEC, Enphase and VARTA.
  • Which FoxESS model is the 22 kW "E3" and which battery modules the "E3" uses (the 12 kW datasheet names both 3.3 kWh and 4.14 kWh); which grid meter "E3" systems have.
  • How many systems EKD sold of each generation.
