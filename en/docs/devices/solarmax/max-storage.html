Device knowledge

These pages are technical. They collect what the OpenAmpere contributors have found out about the devices, each fact with its source and how sure it is. They show the latest state of development, which can be ahead of the version you have installed. They are not official manufacturer information. You don't need them to set up OpenAmpere; the app explains everything you need.

Solarmax MAX.STORAGE ("AMPERE.Storage")

Not official manufacturer information: compiled by the OpenAmpere contributors, can be incomplete or wrong. Changing inverter settings can affect the warranty and grid rules; no affiliation with Solarmax or EKD; not legal advice. See the device knowledge README.

Applies to: Solarmax MAX.STORAGE and MAX.STORAGE Ultimate hybrid inverters · Sold as: "AMPERE.Storage" (EKD, the 10 kW Ultimate, about 2022–2023) · OpenAmpere: driver solarmax (src/openampere/drivers/solarmax/), register map solarmax_max_storage · Support: display only · Confirmed on a real device: no (built from the manufacturer's register list and the simulator)

Summary

  • Modbus TCP on port 502, unit id 1, input registers (FC04), addresses from 0, 32-bit values with the low word first (unlike FoxESS and SAJ).
  • The live values (PV, battery, house, grid, state of charge) are readable on every device. Everything from register 130 on, including the lifetime energy counters, needs "Connectivity+", which only Solarmax unlocked; without it OpenAmpere has no energy history for this device.
  • Signs differ: Solarmax counts battery charging and grid export as positive; the driver turns both around.
  • OpenAmpere only reads. EKD switched the storage function of these devices off remotely in 2025 (names.md); OpenAmpere must never switch it on again.
  • Solarmax is insolvent; the old apps are gone and the portal costs money, so OpenAmpere may be the only free local app left for these owners (#519).

Decisions in OpenAmpere

DecisionWhyIssue/PR
Display only: every write method refuses, supports_control is off.EKD switched the storage function off remotely; control also needs "Connectivity+". OpenAmpere never switches it on again.#515, #519
Detected after FoxESS and SAJ, at unit 1, by reading 100–126 with FC04. The device counts as a Solarmax only if firmware (100–999), device state (0–3), nominal power (1–30 kW), state of charge and capacity (at most 100) are all plausible.Detection must not claim another device; SAJ also answers at unit 1, but not with FC04 at 100.#519
Without the counters of "Connectivity+", PV power is the inverter's own DC power (110); the rest of the plant (112 − 110) is shown as a second PV inverter the owner can count, once it was more than 50 W at least once, or with 0 W as soon as the owner counts one (pv.external). With the counters, PV power is the whole plant's (112) and there is no second inverter to count.112 includes other inverters connected to the MAX.STORAGE; without one the app must not offer to count it. What was seen is not stored, so after a restart at night a counted inverter would otherwise be unknown until the sun comes back. The PV counter (150) already includes the connected inverters: counting one on top would count its energy twice.#519
The energy counters (150–159) are used only when they answer and are not all zero, and are no longer asked for after a refusal, or after three reads in a row without an answer if they never answered (counters that answered before are then asked once a minute); there is no load counter, the consumption is their balance.Without "Connectivity+" the device may refuse them, answer zeros or not answer; zeros would look like counters that start at 0, and the live values must never wait for them.#519
Device state 3 (error) is reported as a fault; 2 (warning) is not.A warning is no fault the owner must act on.#519
The diagnostics show the powers with Solarmax's own signs (rounded) and whether the "Connectivity+" registers answer, without the serial number and without counter values.So the first owner's report can confirm the signs.#519

Open questions

  • Whether port 502 answers with factory settings, and whether 100–126 really read without "Connectivity+".
  • The signs of 114 and 118 on a real device (the list and evcc agree; no owner's report yet).
  • What 130–159 return without "Connectivity+": an exception or zeros.
  • What 103, 114, 123 and 124 show while EKD's switch-off is active, and whether PV keeps producing then.
  • Whether 116 is the whole house consumption when a second inverter is in the house.
  • Which firmware EKD's units run; the list is for 3.4.4 and later.
  • What the type plate says, and whether EKD's units carry "AMPERE" on the housing.
