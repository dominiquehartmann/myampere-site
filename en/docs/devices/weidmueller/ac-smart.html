Device knowledge

These pages are technical. They collect what the OpenAmpere contributors have found out about the devices, each fact with its source and how sure it is. They show the latest state of development, which can be ahead of the version you have installed. They are not official manufacturer information. You don't need them to set up OpenAmpere; the app explains everything you need.

Weidmüller AC SMART ("AMPERE.Charge")

Not official manufacturer information: compiled by the OpenAmpere contributors, can be incomplete or wrong. No affiliation with Weidmüller or EKD; not legal advice. See the device knowledge README.

Applies to: Weidmüller AC SMART ECO, VALUE and ADVANCED wallboxes · Sold as: "AMPERE.Charge" (EKD, the VALUE with 11 kW, about 2023–2024) · OpenAmpere: wallbox module weidmueller_ac_smart (src/openampere/wallbox/weidmueller.py) · Support: display only (live view and the read-only check of the setup) · Confirmed on a real device: no (built from Weidmüller's register document and the simulator)

Summary

  • Modbus TCP is on at delivery: port 502, unit id 255, holding registers (FC03). It accepts only one Modbus client at a time: while evcc or the previous smartbox holds the connection, OpenAmpere gets none.
  • Values of 4 bytes come with the low word first; the document does not say so, evcc found it on real devices.
  • OpenAmpere only shows the wallbox: state, power, current and voltage per phase, the energy of the charge, its limits. It never writes; the only mode is "manual".
  • Control would work as a satellite box (a load management current renewed within a timeout). When the controller goes away, the wallbox charges on with its fallback current, like the FoxESS charger (foxess/ac-charger.md): control needs the same safety rules and a confirmation on a real device first (#523).

"Weidmüller document" is "AC SMART ECO / VALUE / ADVANCED Modbus-Register", Dok.-Nr. 2933050000 Rev. 02 (2023-06), public on Weidmüller's website. It warns not to write the stored (non-volatile) registers cyclically. It lists no registers at 312–316, 433 and 703; OpenAmpere reads no block across them.

Behaviour

Modbus TCP is preconfigured at delivery (port 502, unit id 255) and allows only one Modbus client; Modbus over WLAN instead of LAN is a setting that needs a restart.documented; derived (owners confirm the one-client limit)operating manual "AC SMART ECO/VALUE/ADVANCED"; photovoltaikforum.com thread 227016 (2024)
32-bit values come with the low word first.derivedevcc-io/evcc#15261, evcc-io/evcc#35118 and evcc-io/evcc#35123 (behaviour seen on devices; evcc's charger/weidmüller.go is not MIT, nothing taken from it)
Control as a satellite box: write 11052, then set 11050 again before it runs out (the application note's example: 60 s).documentedapplication note "Einbindung der AC Smart in Lade-/Lastmanagement" (2023-05, attached in evcc-io/evcc#15261)
Without its control box a satellite charges at the minimum of 6 A (manual); evcc saw it fall back about 65 s after evcc stopped. It charges on, it does not stop.documented (manual), derived (evcc)operating manual; evcc-io/evcc#35123
A factory reset sets 11000 back to 0 but keeps 702; with 11000 = 1 and 702 = 0 the wallbox does not charge at all.derivedevcc-io/evcc#35118, evcc-io/evcc#35123
The wallbox can itself read a meter or inverter over Modbus TCP (registers 495, 5606–5677); EKD owners entered inverter addresses there, so it may hold one of the inverter's Modbus connections.documented (registers), derived (EKD use)Weidmüller document; photovoltaikforum.com thread 227016

Weidmüller's "AC SMART App" works over Bluetooth, WLAN or LAN without a cloud account.documentedApp Store listing

Decisions in OpenAmpere

DecisionWhyIssue/PR
Display only: never written, only the mode "manual"; its power counts in the house load like any wallbox.Nothing confirmed on a real device; when OpenAmpere stops, the wallbox would charge on with its fallback current.#523
One short connection per read, closed right away.The wallbox takes only one Modbus client; holding it would lock out the owner's app and other tools.#523; wallbox/modbus.py
A voltage above 400 V, a current above 80 A, a power above 30 kW, a charge above 200 kWh or longer than a week are not shown; with such a power the state is unknown.That is what the other word order would give: better no value than a wrong one.#523

Open questions

  • The word order on an EKD unit, and which firmware they run.
  • What 11054 holds after power-up and whether it can be 0; what the wallbox does when 11050 runs out.
  • Which phase register a satellite follows, 317 or 318.
  • Whether something else (the previous smartbox, evcc) still holds its Modbus connection on EKD systems.
  • The unit of the temperature (432).
  • Whether the wallbox reads the inverter itself on EKD systems (495, 5606) and so holds one of its Modbus connections.
  • Whether the AC SMART App works without the internet.
  • Whether it measures the voltages behind its contactor: then an idle wallbox would show one phase, and the live view should take the installed phases from 319 instead.
