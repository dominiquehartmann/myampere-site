Device knowledge

These pages are technical. They collect what the OpenAmpere contributors have found out about the devices, each fact with its source and how sure it is. They show the latest state of development, which can be ahead of the version you have installed. They are not official manufacturer information. You don't need them to set up OpenAmpere; the app explains everything you need.

Relays, smart plugs and SG Ready

Not official manufacturer information. Compiled by the OpenAmpere contributors; can be incomplete or wrong. No affiliation with the manufacturers named here, not legal advice. See Device knowledge.

Applies to: Shelly relays (Gen1, Gen2 and newer), any device that can be switched by calling a web address, heat pumps with an SG Ready input switched through such a relay · OpenAmpere: consumers, src/openampere/consumers.py (my-PV heating rods: mypv/elwa.md) · Support: switching on and off; their power is not read · Confirmed on a real device: no

Summary

  • These devices are only switched on or off, with a minimum run time and a minimum pause time. OpenAmpere uses the power the owner enters; it does not read their power.
  • Shelly Gen1 and Gen2 relays are switched through their local HTTP interface; any other device through one web address for on and one for off.
  • A relay has no time limit: one that OpenAmpere switched on stays on while OpenAmpere is stopped or uninstalled.
  • For an SG Ready heat pump OpenAmpere only switches the relay on the SG Ready contact; what the heat pump does with the signal is set on the heat pump.
  • None of these devices has been confirmed with OpenAmpere on a real system yet.

Decisions in OpenAmpere

DecisionWhyIssue/PR
Relays and web-address devices are only switched on and off, with minimum run and pause times, and with the power the owner enters; the channel of a Shelly relay is selectable. A web-address device takes one address for on and one for off (http or https).Works with any switchable device without reading it.docs/devices.md
A relay that OpenAmpere really switched on is remembered (meta consumer_switched_on with its configuration, kept across restarts and a backup restore). It is switched off when it is disabled or removed, when its address, channel or type changes, or when test mode is entered.Release only what OpenAmpere switched; switching off is the safe direction.#217, #224, #243, #247
Switching off is written even with the master switch off or in test mode.Leaving a device on is the risk.#217, #243
After a restart OpenAmpere sends the wanted state once; in manual mode it always sends.The relay's state is unknown after a power cut.#243, #247
An unreachable device is tried again after 15 s, 30 s and so on up to every 10 minutes, not on every tick; only the first error is logged. A disabled or removed device that stays unreachable is given up after a day.Avoids flooding the network and the log.#243, #247
When OpenAmpere stops, only adjustable devices (such as a heating rod) are set to 0 W; relays are left as they are. The app and the guides tell owners to set relays to "Aus" (off) on the "Geräte" page before stopping or uninstalling.Relays have no time limit and OpenAmpere cannot switch them after it has stopped.#359, #365
Modbus consumers accept only device addresses 1–247; never the broadcast 0.Behind a gateway every device would take a write to 0.#259, #262

Open questions

  • No relay, web-address device or SG Ready heat pump has been confirmed with OpenAmpere on a real system (#111, #173, #185).
  • #359 brought up that relays stay on after a stop, while relays are still listed as not confirmed on a real system. The fact is shown as derived here until a report from a real relay confirms it.
  • Which SG Ready states a heat pump uses with a single contact depends on the heat pump; OpenAmpere switches one contact only.
