Which EKD devices work with OpenAmpere?

Since 2018, EKD has sold batteries, wallboxes and other devices of several makers. This page shows what OpenAmpere can do with them today and what is being worked on.

Which device you have is on the type plate of the battery, the inverter or the wallbox. The sales name is usually in EKD's offer or invoice.

What “planned” means

Every planned device has an issue on GitHub, but no date yet. Monitoring comes first. Control comes only after an owner has confirmed the values on their system.

Do you have such a device and can test? Then please say so in the issue: which device according to the type plate. Leave out the serial number, because the issue is public.

Details and sources for every device are in the device overview in the repository.
